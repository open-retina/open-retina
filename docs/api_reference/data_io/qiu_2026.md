# Qiu et al. 2026 Dataset

Mouse retinal ganglion cell axonal bouton responses to natural movies, recorded with chronic two-photon
calcium imaging in the superficial layers of the superior colliculus of awake mice, originally published in
Qiu et al. (2026):
[The functional organization of retinal input to the mouse superior colliculus](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.04.15.718783v1),
bioRxiv.

Dataset: [huggingface.co/datasets/open-retina/open-retina](https://huggingface.co/datasets/open-retina/open-retina/tree/main/franke_lab/qiu_2026)
— ten sessions from three mice (~17k boutons after quality masking), one zip per session under
`franke_lab/qiu_2026/`.

Train on it with `uv run openretina train --config-name qiu_2026_core_readout`.

## Dataset layout

Unlike the other datasets shipped with openretina, `qiu_2026` arrives in the Sensorium FileTree format: one
`.npy` per trial at `data/<stream>/{i}.npy` for the four streams `videos`, `responses`, `behavior` and
`pupil_center`, positionally aligned to the rows of `meta/trials/*.npy` and NaN-padded at the end to a
length that is uniform within a session but differs between them (300, 450 or 960 frames); a clip trial's
valid portion is always its first 300 frames, i.e. 10 s at 30 Hz. The [trial helpers](#trial-helpers) hold the
shared trimming, splitting and session-discovery logic that keeps all four streams frame-aligned.

Four things set this dataset apart from the others:

- **Behavior is folded into the input channels.** The model input is `(3, T, 36, 64)`: one grayscale video
  channel plus two behavior channels (pupil size and locomotion) broadcast across every pixel. The video
  channel is z-scored with the session's shipped scalar statistics, each behavior channel with its own, so
  the `norm_mean`/`norm_std` on the returned `MoviesTrainTestSplit` describe the video channel only.
- **Targets are raw calcium fluorescence,** masked per session with the shipped `neurons_fluor_good` array
  (an *index* array, not a boolean mask). `spike_inference` selects the transform: `"raw"` leaves the trace
  untouched, `"subtract_min"` shifts each neuron by its train-derived minimum to give the Poisson loss a
  non-negative target, and `"cascade"` is reserved for inferred spike rates and not yet implemented.
- **Pupil center is loaded separately** by [`pupil`](#pupil). It is not a model target: it feeds the
  shifter, which corrects each neuron's readout center for eye movements. Because openretina's shared
  `DataPoint` carries only `(inputs, targets)`, this subpackage defines its own `QiuDataPoint` and
  `QiuMovieDataSet` rather than changing the shared one.
- **Test clips are repeated within a session,** but the design is not the same in every session: six
  sessions share one set of six clips at 14–20 repeats each, while the four with the least training data
  were tested on 94–96 distinct clips at 1–10 repeats (supersets of those same six). Repeats are keyed by
  `condition_hash`. The video is identical across a condition's repeats but the behavior and eye position
  are not, and the model is nonlinear in both, so with `per_repeat_test_behavior=True` the test dataloader
  puts the repeats in the batch dimension and averages the *predictions* rather than the inputs. Non-clip
  trials in the same `test` tier — `chirp`/`moving bar` movies in five sessions, `monet2`/`trippy` noise in
  the other four — are excluded by the `stimulus_type="clip"` filter. The
  [dataset page](../../package_docs/datasets/qiu_2026.md) has the per-session breakdown.
